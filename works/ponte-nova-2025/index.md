# Ponte Nova

https://renataalberigi.com.br/works/ponte-nova-2025/

**Artist:** Renata Alberigi
**Year:** 2025
**Medium:** Oil on canvas
**Dimensions:** 120 × 80 cm
**Original title:** Ponte Nova

Field study: Ponte Velha, Chapada Diamantina, Bahia, Brazil.
