# RUADOFLOW — Brand Design Collection

https://contato675.github.io/renata-alberigi-portfolio/works/ruadoflow-brand-design-2024-2025/

**Artist:** Renata Alberigi
**Collection period:** 2024–2025
**Medium:** Illustration and apparel design
**Original title:** RUADOFLOW — Brand Design Collection

A complete collection drawn by Renata Alberigi for RUADOFLOW between 2024 and 2025 and officially launched on the brand’s website. The images present her designs on T-shirts and hoodies.
